Veterinary Career Guide

Flagship
vetcareerguide.com ↗

A subscription commerce and publishing platform carried entirely by one custom WordPress plugin I wrote from scratch, plus a theme built specifically for it. Elementor handles public pages only; the plugin drives everything else, and programmatically controls both Elementor and Formidable Forms.

  • Custom subscription billing — written directly against the Paystack, Flutterwave and Stripe APIs rather than their built-in subscription products, with WP Cron tracking each member's billing date and charging on schedule
  • Geo-routed payments — the gateway is selected automatically by visitor country
  • Multi-currency — prices held in Naira and converted from the visitor's detected location, with a manual override
  • Fulfilment — per-buyer ebook watermarking; hardcopy purchases redeemed through unique codes printed in the physical book
  • Member dashboard — progress tracker with calendar, to-do list, habit tracker and goal tracking
  • Discounting — standard, group and email-targeted coupons, early-bird pricing and stock management, each with an admin screen so non-technical staff run promotions without a developer

AFIWEL — Africa Fish & Aquaculture Welfare

afiwel.com ↗

Full site plus a Resources plugin now serving 59 publications across an 11-country programme. One plugin replaced four: it registers its own custom post type and fields in place of ACF, exposes itself as a custom Elementor widget so editors compose pages normally, and owns its query, filtering and pagination — which is why it survives Elementor updates and shifting post IDs. Every resource auto-generates its type badge, View and Download actions, and share links for eight platforms on publish.

Its news archive ran on three or four stacked filter plugins; I replaced them with one I wrote — Year, Category and keyword filtering, available as both a shortcode and an Elementor widget, owning its own query, results and pagination.
